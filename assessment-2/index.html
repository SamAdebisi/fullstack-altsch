FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and creating a shorter, condensed version that redirects to the original URL. When a user clicks on the shortened link, they are redirected to the intended destination.

Is it necessary to create an account to use the URL shortening service?

Just trying things out? Check the sign-up requirements for your chosen plan. An account is handy for keeping your links and analytics in one place.

Are the shortened links permanent? Will they expire?

A short link’s lifetime depends on the service and plan behind it. Before putting one on a poster or business card, check its expiry settings and keep the destination page available.

Are there any limitations on the number of URLs I can shorten?

Your plan sets the pace. Basic includes unlimited URL shortening; compare the other plan features to find the right fit for your next project.

Can I customize the shortened URLs to reflect my brand or content?

Give your link a little personality with a memorable custom alias. For a more branded touch, the Professional plan includes custom branded domains.

Can I track the performance of my shortened URLs?

Yes—link analytics help you see which links get attention. Start with Basic Link Analytics, or explore Professional for a closer look at your link performance.

How secure is the URL shortening service? Are the shortened links protected against spam or malicious activity?

A tidy link still deserves a careful click. Only share destinations you trust, avoid entering sensitive details on unfamiliar pages, and check the service’s security policy for its specific protections.

What is a QR code and what can it do?

Think of a QR code as a scannable doorway. Point a phone camera at it to open a menu, event page, portfolio, or another link without typing the address.

Is there an API available for integrating the URL shortening service into my own applications or websites?

API access is included in the Teams plan. It’s the option to explore when you want link shortening to become part of your own app or website.
